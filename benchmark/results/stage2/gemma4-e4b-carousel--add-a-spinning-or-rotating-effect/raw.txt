I changed the animation duration for the `.carousel-container` to make the spinning effect more pronounced, changing it from `20s` to `12s`.

Specific change:
*   On line 11, in the `.carousel-container` CSS rule, I changed `animation: spin 20s linear infinite;` to `animation: spin 12s linear infinite;`.
