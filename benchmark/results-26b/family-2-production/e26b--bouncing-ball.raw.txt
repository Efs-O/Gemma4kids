Woohoo! 🥳 That is a super fun idea! A bouncing ball is a perfect way to start learning about animations. 

I've created a bright, bouncy red ball for you! It even has a little shine on it to make it look 3D, and I added some fluffy clouds and a green floor so it has a beautiful place to bounce. ☁️🌱

Look at how it uses `ease-in` and `ease-out`—that's a secret coding trick to make the ball slow down at the top and speed up as it hits the ground, just like a real ball!

Do you want to try changing the color of the ball to your favorite color? You can look for `radial-gradient` in the code! 🎨✨
